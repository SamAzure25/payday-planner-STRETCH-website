ABOUT STRETCH

Your money has to last longer than payday.

Stretch was created around a simple everyday problem: knowing you have money today is not the same as knowing how much you can safely spend today.

THE IDEA

Money management shouldn't feel like a guessing game.

Between one payday and the next, there are groceries, transport, bills, unexpected expenses and everyday spending to think about.

It can be difficult to know whether the money in your account needs to last five days, ten days or several weeks — especially when you're making calculations in your head.

Stretch is designed to make that part simpler by bringing your money, expenses and payday together in one place.
