GENERAL

About Stretch

Stretch is a payday planner designed to help you make your money last until your next payday. You provide information about your available money, expenses and payday, and Stretch helps you understand how much you can safely spend each day.

Stretch is designed for anyone who wants a simpler way to plan their spending between paydays. It can be useful if you regularly find yourself wondering how much money you can spend today while still covering the days ahead.

No. Stretch is a planning and budgeting tool. It does not replace your bank account and does not hold your money.

HOW IT WORKS

Using Stretch

Stretch looks at the information you provide, such as the money available to you, planned expenses and the number of days until payday. It then uses those figures to give you a daily spending guide.

Adding or changing your planned expenses can affect the amount available for the remaining days. Stretch is designed to help you see how those changes affect your spending plan.

Stretch is built around your next payday, so keeping your payday information up to date is important. The available options may depend on the version of Stretch you are using.

It is Stretch's estimate of the amount you can spend today while still accounting for the information you have entered for the days leading up to payday. It is a planning guide, not a guarantee.

YOUR MONEY

Money & Expenses

Stretch is designed around the information you provide rather than requiring access to your bank account. You enter the relevant amounts so Stretch can use them for your planning calculations.

You should enter the expenses that you want Stretch to account for in your plan. The more accurate the information you provide, the more useful your spending guidance can be.

Your plan is based on the information available at the time. If your spending changes, updating your information can help you get a more relevant picture of the days remaining until payday.
